# Glossary

Industry-specific terms used throughout this add-on. Parent-playbook terminology is documented in [`../../docs/08-glossary.md`](../../../docs/08-glossary.md) and is not duplicated here.

## Search and Hiring

**ADEA** — Age Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967. US federal anti-discrimination statute prohibiting age-based employment discrimination for workers 40+. Active enforcement against AI hiring tools; *Mobley v. Workday* conditionally certified as ADEA collective on May 16, 2025.

**AEDT** — Automated Employment Decision Tool. The defined category in NYC Local Law 144 covering tools that "substantially assist or replace discretionary decision making" in hiring or promotion.

**Board search** — A retained-search engagement specifically for board-of-director seats. Visually distinct from executive search (more austere register, more confidentiality, different deliverable formats).

**Candidate dossier** — The long-form document a retained-search firm prepares for a client search committee summarizing a candidate's career arc, signature wins, references, and fit assessment. Confidentiality conventions are strict.

**EVP** — Employer Value Proposition. The articulated promise an employer makes to candidates and employees about what working there is like. Visually expressed via three-to-five-pillar systems (Marriott "Be," CAE "Our Promise. For All.").

**Longlist** — In retained search, a longer list of potential candidates produced early in the process before narrowing to a shortlist. Often anonymized for client review.

**RPO** — Recruitment Process Outsourcing. Firms that take over a client's recruiting function as a service (Cielo, Sevenstep, KellyOCG, Allegis).

**Slate** — In retained search, the final group of candidates (typically 3-5) presented to the client search committee. Synonym: shortlist.

**Succession** — Planning for executive transitions, typically internal. Distinct from search (often handled by the same firms but with different deliverables).

## Talent Intelligence Platform

**Capability map** — A visualization of an organization's skill inventory, often as a heatmap or radial diagram. The platform-side equivalent of an org chart for skills.

**Force-directed graph** — A network visualization where nodes are skills/roles/people and edges are relationships, with an algorithm placing them in spatial relationship to each other. Signature visual for skills intelligence platforms.

**Internal mobility** — The movement of employees between roles within an organization. Platforms like Gloat and Eightfold are organized around this concept.

**Loomra** — Gloat's Knowledge Graph product. Distinct visual identity (orbital animation).

**O*NET / Lightcast Open Skills** — Standard taxonomies that platforms standardize on. Lightcast Open Skills (33,000+ skills, refreshed bi-weekly) is the open standard. O*NET is the US Department of Labor reference.

**Sankey diagram** — A flow visualization where ribbons of varying width show movement between source and destination categories. The canonical visual for talent flow / migration.

**Skills graph** — The platform-side data structure relating skills, roles, and people. Visually rendered as a force-directed graph.

**Skills inference** — Algorithmic derivation of an employee's skills from their employment history, education, and other signals (Eightfold's flagship capability).

**Talent flow** — Movement of talent between companies, geographies, or roles. Visualized as Sankey diagrams or arc maps.

**Workforce planning** — The strategic discipline of matching workforce capacity to organizational need over time. Platform-side often visualized as scenario dashboards.

## Compliance

**C2PA** — Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity. The standards body publishing the technical specification for cryptographically-signed content credentials. Adopted by Adobe, Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, Meta, Amazon, and major camera manufacturers.

**Content Credentials** — The brand name for C2PA-signed metadata, surfaced as a "CR" icon in supporting platforms.

**DPIA** — Data Protection Impact Assessment. GDPR Article 35 obligation triggered by certain high-risk processing including AI systems trained on personal data.

**EEOC** — Equal Employment Opportunity Commission. The US federal agency enforcing anti-discrimination law in employment.

**ELVIS Act** — Tennessee's "Ensuring Likeness Voice and Image Security Act," effective July 1, 2024. Civil and Class A misdemeanor liability for unauthorized AI replicas of voice or likeness.

**EU AI Act** — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. High-risk obligations for recruitment-and-selection AI applicable August 2, 2026 under Annex III §4.

**Mobley v. Workday** — Class-action lawsuit alleging Workday's AI-screening tool produced age, race, and disability discrimination. Conditionally certified as ADEA collective on May 16, 2025; potential class size in the hundreds of millions of applicants.

**Monk Skin Tone Scale** — A 10-point skin tone scale developed by Dr. Ellis Monk and adopted by Google for representation testing. Used in this add-on's representation-testing protocols (see [`compliance/representation-testing.md`](../compliance/representation-testing.md)).

**NYC Local Law 144** — Effective July 5, 2023. Requires bias audits and candidate notice for AEDTs used in NYC hiring. Limited scope (does not directly cover imagery, but covers programmatic ad-targeting that selects which images to show which candidates).

**OFCCP** — Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs. US federal agency overseeing affirmative action and EEO compliance for federal contractors. April 29, 2024 AI guidance is the most current federal touchstone.

**SynthID** — Google DeepMind's invisible watermarking technology. Embedded in 20B+ images by Google AI tools as of 2026. Distinct from C2PA but complementary.

**Title VII** — Civil Rights Act of 1964 Title VII. US federal anti-discrimination statute covering race, color, religion, sex, and national origin.

## Production / Workflow

**Archival quality (Tier 3)** — The production tier for executive search dossiers, board indexes, annual workforce reports. 6-24 weeks. Specialist agencies plus senior in-house design. Low or forbidden AI imagery.

**Bento grid** — A modular tile layout combining a giant numeral, delta arrow, mini-chart, and chip cloud. Dominant 2025-2026 marketing-graphic convention.

**Choropleth map** — A map where geographic regions are colored by a data variable. The canonical visual for global supply/demand in talent intelligence.

**Fast iteration (Tier 1)** — The production tier for LinkedIn recruiter posts, job posting headers, internal recruiting comms. Hours-to-days. In-house Canva/Figma component libraries. Heavy AI use.

**Mid-tier (Tier 2)** — The production tier for career-site refreshes, employer-brand campaigns, conference materials, EVP launch toolkits. 4-12 weeks. Agency-led with in-house brand stewardship. Moderate AI use.

**Squircle** — A square with rounded corners, halfway between a square and a circle. Robert Half's signature image-mask shape.

**Touch illustration** — Randstad's term for its illustration library — flat-vector illustrations of human-tech interaction (robots, rockets, compasses) functioning as authenticity hedges.

## Brand and Awards

**ATAP** — Association of Talent Acquisition Professionals.

**Brandon Hall** — Long-running awards program recognizing excellence in HR / learning / talent management.

**EBM Awards** — Employer Brand Management Awards (UK / international).

**ERE Awards** — ERE Recruitment Awards (recruitment industry, North America).

**Fosway 9-Grid** — Fosway Group's analyst recognition framework for HR Tech vendors. Visual currency on platform marketing pages.

**IDC MarketScape** — IDC analyst recognition framework. Visual currency.

**RAD Awards** — Recruitment Advertising and Design Awards (UK).

**Rally Awards** — Rally Recruitment Marketing Awards (North America).

**Recognition badges** — The collected analyst, awards, and certification badges (Fosway, IDC, Brandon Hall, Top HR Products) displayed on platform marketing materials.

**SHRM** — Society for Human Resource Management. Largest US HR professional association.

**Universum** — Global employer-branding research and ranking firm; "Most Attractive Employer" study referenced as authority.
