## Compliance Posture

This category produces some of the highest-stakes confidential deliverables in the talent industry. Specific compliance attention:

- **Confidentiality.** Project codenames must be real and project-specific. "STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL" stamps are non-decorative.
- **No real-person likeness in AI generation.** Executive bio plates representing real candidates require real photography with consent. AI-generated portraits in this slot are prohibited (see [`../../compliance/consent-and-likeness.md`](../../compliance/consent-and-likeness.md)).
- **Anonymization conventions.** Market maps use industry-standard gray silhouettes, hatched-circle company logo replacements, and role-and-industry descriptors only.
- **Five-year recordkeeping.** Per [`../../compliance/ai-imagery-policy.md`](../../compliance/ai-imagery-policy.md) Section 9.
