# Sub-Register A — Retained-Search Editorial

Visual specification for the retained-search editorial register. The world of Korn Ferry, Spencer Stuart, Egon Zehnder, Russell Reynolds, and Heidrick & Struggles.

## At a Glance

The editorial restraint of *Foreign Affairs* applied to executive talent. Single-accent palette, generous white space, soft documentary portraiture, serif or humanist-sans typography, architectural metaphor photography. Charts use one firm color plus one neutral. Numbers are rendered very large. Everything declares "this is for grown-ups."

## Palette System

A single firm-defining accent color, plus a neutral system (typically deep navy/black, warm ivory/off-white, sometimes a warm gray mid-tone).

### BHIL Parent-Brand Mapping

The parent BHIL register (navy `#0F1A2E`, ivory `#F5EFE3`, amber `#C9A24A`, signal-red `#A82A2E`) maps directly onto sub-register A. The parent register is essentially Russell Reynolds navy + Heidrick warmth + a single signal-red accent. Prompts in this register can use the parent BHIL profile and srefs unchanged.

### Palette Discipline

- **One accent, not two.** Spencer Stuart never pairs its red with a second loud color; the discipline is the brand. Departing from this discipline reads as off-register.
- **Neutrals carry weight.** The dominant compositional gesture is white space. Neutrals are 80%+ of the visual surface; the accent is a 5-15% component.
- **Color in charts is one firm color plus one neutral.** Three or four colors in a chart reads as "consultancy slide," not "retained-search dossier."

## Typography

The register has settled on two typographic camps:

### Camp 1 — Humanist Sans

Korn Ferry, Russell Reynolds, Heidrick. The KF Serif bracket display + a humanist sans body. The sans is geometric but warm — Akkurat, Greycliff, GT America in their humanist cuts.

### Camp 2 — Serif Editorial

Spencer Stuart (Rotis Serif Regular as the historical signature), Egon Zehnder. Editorial display serif paired with a sans body. Canela Deck and GT Sectra Bold in the BHIL parent register sit in this camp.

### Type Discipline

- **Display headlines large and centered or left-aligned.** No center-rag-tight settings; no all-caps body type; no character-spacing tricks.
- **Body type small but generous in line-height.** 9-10pt body with 14-15pt leading is the comfortable register. Line lengths short (45-65 characters per line).
- **Numbers very large.** A data plate leads with a 200pt+ percentage in firm color, followed by 2-3 lines of 14-16pt interpretive text.
- **Small caps for labels.** Practice areas, series names, and section labels are typically rendered in tracked small caps ("CEO & BOARD OF DIRECTORS PRACTICE").

## Photography Conventions

The register's photography is **soft, documentary, slightly desaturated, and restrained**. Three contexts:

### Executive Portrait

- **Lighting:** soft single-source key at 45°, fill at 30% main intensity. Natural-window or large softbox aesthetic. Background 1-2 stops darker than face.
- **Framing:** tight head-and-shoulders or chest-up. Eyeline at upper third of frame. Direct-to-camera gaze (occasional thoughtful off-camera for editorial features).
- **Wardrobe:** tailored business attire. No pattern louder than fine pinstripe. Squared shoulders, subtle forward lean, hands typically not visible.
- **Background:** blurred boardroom, neutral wall, or pure neutral field. Egon Zehnder skews toward near-pure black or pure white backgrounds. Spencer Stuart toward editorial environments. Korn Ferry toward aspirational "looking upward" lifestyle shots.

### Architectural Metaphor

- **Subject:** building exterior, mountain summit, city skyline, abstract structural geometry.
- **Time of day:** typically early morning or late afternoon for warm directional light. Heidrick's *Route to the Top* covers consistently use mountain summit imagery.
- **Treatment:** slightly desaturated, slightly warm cast, fine grain. Reads as "documentary photo book" not "stock photo."

### Document and Data Detail

- **Subject:** annotated documents, archival photography, data plates as subject.
- **Treatment:** crisp focus, paper-grain texture, slightly warm cast. Often used as section dividers or chapter openers.

## Data Visualization Conventions

### Charts

- **One firm color plus one neutral.** No more.
- **Hairline rules.** 0.5-0.75pt rules in warm gray for axes and gridlines.
- **No drop shadows.** No 3D effects. No gradient fills in chart bars.
- **Numbers prominently labeled.** Data points labeled with values; legends minimal.

### Data Plates

The signature deliverable component. A single oversize percentage or number, followed by interpretive text:

```
[200pt+ percentage in firm accent color]

[Interpretive subhead in 14-16pt body type, 1-3 lines]

[Hairline rule in warm gray]

[Source / methodology / caption in 8-9pt small type]
```

Spencer Stuart's *US Board Index* uses this template throughout.

### Maps and Geographic

- Choropleth maps in single-hue gradients (firm color from light to saturated).
- Country/region labels in small caps.
- No flag iconography or stereotypical national imagery.

## Layout Conventions

### Cover Plate

- Full-bleed firm-color field OR architectural metaphor photograph OR minimal abstract data motif as the dominant visual.
- Small-caps practice/series label top-left ("CEO & BOARD OF DIRECTORS PRACTICE").
- Editorial display title in serif or humanist sans.
- Year/edition tag in the lower zone (e.g., "2025 · 40th Edition").
- Logo small in the corner.

### Internal Spread

- Left-page display quote or oversize stat.
- Right-page two-column body.
- Sidebar callouts in firm-color tinted background or in a hairline-ruled box.
- Generous outer margins; modest inner margins.

### Section Divider

- Full-bleed firm-color field.
- White display numeral (the section number) at large scale.
- White display title.
- Sometimes a tracked-small-caps section identifier above the title.

## Off-Register Signals

Three signals mark imagery as off-register for sub-register A:

1. **Bright multi-color palettes.** Reads as tech startup or B2B SaaS; not retained search.
2. **3D charts with drop shadows.** Reads as PowerPoint; not editorial publication.
3. **Stock handshake / diversity-grid photography.** Reads as outsourced HR communication; not retained search.

## Production Implications for Prompts

Prompts targeting sub-register A:

- Use the parent BHIL profile and srefs unchanged (or adapt to the specific firm palette).
- Default to architectural metaphor photography over portraiture for cover plates.
- Default to environmental detail or anonymous-silhouette over face-forward portraiture for any imagery that might be mistaken for a real candidate.
- Use data plates as standalone visual elements (large numeral + short interpretive text) rather than embedded in dense layouts.
- Use small caps for practice/series labels.
- Use serif or humanist-sans typography; avoid display sans-serif (Bebas, Anton, etc.) which read as off-register.
