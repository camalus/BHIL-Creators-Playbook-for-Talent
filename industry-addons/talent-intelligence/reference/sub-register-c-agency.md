# Sub-Register C — Recruiting-Agency Editorial-Illustration Hybrid

Visual specification for the recruiting-agency editorial-illustration hybrid register. The world of Randstad, Adecco, Robert Half, ManpowerGroup, AMS, and Cielo.

## At a Glance

The middle ground between retained-search austerity and platform dashboard maximalism. Lower-case sentence-case headlines. Custom typefaces as luxury cue. Documentary-daylight portraiture in real workplace contexts. Illustration libraries as authenticity hedges. Single-color chapter dividers and section breaks.

## Palette System

Each major agency owns a distinctive primary color, used boldly. The rest of the palette is restrained: white or near-white grounds, occasional warm-gray mid-tones, and the primary color in confident applications.

### BHIL Parent-Brand Mapping

Sub-register C requires deviation from the parent BHIL register. The parent is too austere for this register's warmer documentary feel. Prompts in this register either:

- Adapt the parent register by adding a warm secondary accent (a coral or warm orange beyond the parent's signal-red), or
- Adopt the client agency's specific palette (Randstad blue, Adecco red, etc.) declared per project as a `[CLIENT BRAND]` set.

## Typography

The register's typographic signature is **lower-case sentence-case headlines**. Randstad, AMS, and increasingly Adecco lead with this convention. It signals approachability, contemporary register, and confidence — "we don't need to shout."

### Display

- **Custom typefaces preferred:** Randstad Sans, Adecco Sans, Robert Half's coral-accented serif. Custom typefaces function as a luxury cue and a brand-distinction signal.
- **Open-source alternatives** when custom isn't available: Inter, Söhne, GT America, Aktiv Grotesk, or Apercu.
- **Sentence case for headlines.** "your future starts here." rather than "YOUR FUTURE STARTS HERE."
- **Body sentence case as well.** Title-case feels off-register.
- **Display weights are bold or extrabold;** body weights are regular.

### Headline Convention

Lower-case sentence-case headlines, often with a period at the end. Examples:

- "your career, your way." (Randstad register)
- "find work that works for you." (Adecco register)
- "let's redefine work together." (AMS register)

The period is consistent — it signals statement, not question or exclamation. The lower-case is consistent — it signals approachability.

### Body Type

- Same family as the display, in regular weight.
- Generous line-height (1.5-1.7×).
- Comfortable line-length (45-65 characters).

## Photography Conventions

The register's photography is **documentary, daylight, distributed-workforce**. Not the executive-search soft single-source key; not the platform abstract data-art. Real workers in real workplaces.

### Career Site / Recruiter-Tile Portrait

- **Lighting:** natural daylight or open shade. Window light dominant; mixed sources acceptable. Slightly cool color temperature.
- **Framing:** off-center, candid, with copy room to the left or right of the subject. Subject often looking away from camera (thoughtful, mid-action) rather than direct-to-camera.
- **Wardrobe:** real workplace attire — what the person actually wears at work, not a styled-for-the-shoot suit. Lab coats, scrubs, branded-tee, hi-vis, business casual.
- **Background:** the subject's actual workplace — office, factory floor, hospital corridor, outdoor site. Not blurred to abstraction; the workplace is part of the story.

### Workplace Detail (No Faces)

- **Subject:** a workspace, a window onto a city, a tooling close-up, a team artifact (whiteboard, sticky-notes, model), a hands-on-keyboard composition.
- **Treatment:** crisp focus, slight warm cast, naturalistic.
- **Use:** job posting heroes, secondary career-site plates, EVP pillar supporting imagery.

### Group Photography

- **Documentary group shots** of real teams at real moments — meetings, lunches, off-sites, work events.
- **Not posed line-ups.** Posed "diverse team smiling at camera" reads as stock.
- **Consent for each identifiable individual** required.

### Anchor Examples

- Atlassian "Team Anywhere" location-stamped portraits.
- Airbnb "Live & Work Anywhere" distributed imagery.
- GitLab Handbook public photo briefs.
- Mollie commissioned signature photographer-filter.
- Stories Incorporated framework (direct-eye, candid, off-center).

## Illustration Conventions

The register's illustration libraries are **authenticity hedges and localization tools**. They sidestep the diversity-grid problem and localize cheaply across markets.

### Randstad's "Touch Illustration" Library

Flat-vector illustrations of human-tech interaction — robots, rockets, compasses, hands holding tools, digital-physical metaphors. Used as accent imagery alongside or instead of photography.

### Adecco's Geometric Chapter Dividers

Solid red chapter divider pages with geometric ornament — diagonals, circles, cropped shapes. Functional rather than illustrated.

### Cielo's Torch Motif

The "Illuminate Talent" brand uses a torch motif as the recurring graphic element, integrated into pattern systems and chapter openers.

### AMS Geometric Data Art

The most adventurous: high-contrast magenta-on-black geometric compositions that abstract data into visual art. Used as cover art and chapter imagery.

### Open Illustration Systems

Several brands have moved to fully illustrated registers as the primary convention:

- **Mailchimp** — Collins-designed "outsider art" illustrations.
- **GitLab** — open-source illustration system, Tanuki + broken-line motif.
- **GitHub** — Octocat character.
- **Notion** — hand-illustrated peach/cream system.
- **Canva** — Canvanaut gradient illustrations.
- **Salesforce** — Astro Trailblazer characters.

These function as durable brand assets that can be deployed without consent, localization, or representation testing concerns. They are the lowest-friction option for many recruiting marketing contexts.

## Layout Conventions

### Career Site Hero

- Full-bleed daylight portrait (preferred — gold standard).
- Headline overlaid in lower third, lower-case sentence-case, white type with subtle shadow.
- CTA button in agency primary color.
- Subhead below headline in 14-16pt body type.

### Recruiter Tile (LinkedIn / Social)

- Brand-color block (full-bleed primary color) covering 50-70% of the tile.
- Pull-quote in display type, white against the brand color.
- Small headshot inset in the remaining 30-50% of the tile.
- Attribution (name, role, location) in 10-12pt body type.

### Salary Guide Cover

- Year as typographic anchor (large numerals — "2026" at 200pt+).
- Market-condition subtitle in display type.
- Brand-color wave or abstract motif as supporting graphic.
- Logo small in upper-left.

### EVP Pillar Treatment

- 3-5 pillar grid layout.
- Each pillar: custom icon at top, headline in display type, supporting copy in body type, supporting image (illustration or photograph) below.
- Marriott "Be" framework (begin / belong / become) is the canonical reference.

## Off-Register Signals

Three signals mark imagery as off-register for sub-register C:

1. **All-caps display headlines.** Title-case is acceptable; ALL-CAPS reads as 2010s direct-response, not contemporary recruiting.
2. **Stock "diverse team smiling at camera" photography.** Documented failure mode (Levi's, Mango Teen). Real workplace documentary or illustration alternative are the legitimate options.
3. **Pure black grounds.** This register is daylight-warm, not dashboard-dark. Dark-mode dashboard imagery reads as platform marketing (sub-register B), not recruiting agency.

## Production Implications for Prompts

Prompts targeting sub-register C:

- Declare a `[CLIENT BRAND]` set per project for agency-specific work.
- Default to documentary daylight photography for human imagery (real photography preferred over AI generation).
- Default to lower-case sentence-case headlines.
- Use illustration alternatives (GitLab register, Mailchimp register, Octocat register) for low-stakes contexts where real photography isn't feasible.
- Avoid AI-generated portraits of fabricated employees (the documented failure mode).
- Avoid all-caps display typography.
- Avoid pure black grounds (use daylight or warm grounds).
