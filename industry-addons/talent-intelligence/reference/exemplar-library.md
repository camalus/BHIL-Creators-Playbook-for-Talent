## How To Use The Library

For prompt construction:

1. Identify the closest 2-3 exemplars to your project.
2. Read their visual language carefully — what colors, what typography, what photography conventions, what data-viz primitives.
3. Reference them in your prompt's "anchor reference" notes (often as `--sref` slots in Midjourney prompts, or as text descriptors in other models).
4. Avoid copying any exemplar literally — use them as anchors for register, not as templates to imitate.

## Update Cadence

This library is curated. New exemplars are added on a quarterly cadence as new benchmark work emerges. Contributions follow the parent repo's contribution process.
